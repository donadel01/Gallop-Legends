# Gallop Legends Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

This policy explains what information Gallop Legends Horse Racing ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have.

## Summary

- The game does not need an account and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
- Your career progress is saved only on your own device.
- The game shows rewarded video ads through **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect some device data, as described below.
- The optional **Gallop Premium** subscription is sold and processed by **Google Play**. The developer never sees your payment details.

## 1. Data saved on your device

Your stable, horses, player name, stable name, jockey silks, settings and game progress are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to the developer. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads, for example when you choose to watch an ad to receive in-game gold support. Ads are not shown to Gallop Premium subscribers.

When an ad is requested, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

- **Device identifiers**, such as the Android Advertising ID
- **Approximate location**, derived from your IP address
- **App interactions**, such as ad views and clicks
- **Crash logs and diagnostics** related to ad delivery and performance

This data is collected by Google, not by the developer. It is used for advertising and measuring ad performance, analytics, and fraud prevention and security. It is sent over an encrypted connection.

To learn how Google uses this data, see:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choices:** You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). Where required by law, for example in the EEA and the UK, you may be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

## 3. Gallop Premium subscription (Google Play Billing)

Gallop Premium is an optional monthly subscription purchased through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and your payment information, under the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock the premium features. The developer does not receive or store your card or payment details.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store app under **Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions**.

## 4. In-game gold

Gold in the game is virtual currency. It can be earned by playing, by watching optional ads, or as a daily bonus with Gallop Premium. It cannot be sold, transferred or exchanged for real money.

## 5. Device permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or files. It uses internet access to load ads and check the subscription status. It may use optional vibration, which you can turn off in Settings.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

The developer does not store your personal data on any server. Progress saved on your device can be deleted by clearing the game's data or uninstalling the game. To manage data collected by Google for advertising, use your device's ad settings or visit https://myadcenter.google.com.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

## 9. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
